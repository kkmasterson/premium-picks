# Soccer Midfielder UI

> **Analysis role:** Midfielder
>
> **Visible designation:** M
>
> **Selected market:** Shots Attempted
>
> **Reference completeness:** Core role presentation confirmed

## Role adaptation

The midfielder retains the shared soccer player shell, offer strip, contextual tabs, and team matchup panels. The role difference is expressed through market priority, selected supporting categories, and the active average card rather than a new page layout.

## Market navigation

The visible portion of the shared soccer market row includes Minutes, Goals, Goals + Assists, Shots, Shots on Target, Pass Attempts, Passes, Touches, Possession, Opponent Possession, Assists, Crosses, and additional action categories beyond the crop.

- `SHOTS` is active and underlined.
- A count badge appears beside Shots and Shots on Target.
- The readable section heading expands the market to `Shots Attempted`.

## Controls and filters

- The selected line uses the shared stepper and provider control.
- Filters visibly include Opponent, League, Home/Away, and Team.
- L5, L10, L15, season, and H2H summary tiles remain in the shared positions.

## Shots Attempted chart

- One bar represents one match.
- The number of attempted shots is displayed above each bar.
- Green represents results above the selected line.
- A zero-result below the line is represented by a short red bar rather than disappearing from the chart.
- The dashed threshold and match date/opponent labels retain the shared soccer chart treatment.

Unlike the goalkeeper saves chart, the midfielder's selected metric does not need a stacked component bar.

## Supporting stats

The visible midfielder supporting categories are:

- Minutes.
- Shots on Target.
- Goals.

Each category displays an average. The selected Minutes chart uses neutral gray bars and makes partial versus full-match playing time immediately visible.

## Contextual right column

- Line Movement and Prop History use the shared top card.
- Primary tabs remain Matchup, Similar, Lineups, and Injuries.
- Secondary tabs remain Odds, Stats, and Form.
- The visible Matchup state uses a round-labeled win predictor and team odds.
- The Averages card uses a recent-game sample plus Home, Away, and opponent accordion rows.

## Remaining reference needed

- Game log.
- Similar or Lineups only if midfielder content differs from other field players.
- Confirmation of any midfielder-specific market grouping beyond the shared row.
