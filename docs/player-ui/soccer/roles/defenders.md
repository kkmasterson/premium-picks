## Role adaptation

The defender uses the shared soccer shell while emphasizing defensive and discipline-related markets. The role is shown beside the player name, and the selected market changes the chart and supporting-stat selector without changing page structure.

## Market navigation

The later portion of the visible soccer market row includes Touches, Possession, Opponent Possession, Assists, Crosses, shot attempts, Clearances, Tackles, Fouls Drawn, Saves, Goals Allowed, dribbles, Fouls, and additional abbreviated categories.

- `FOUL` is active and underlined.
- Count badges appear beside Tackles, Fouls Drawn, and Fouls where multiple variants are available.
- The readable section heading is `Fouls`.

## Fouls chart

- One bar represents one match.
- Exact foul count appears above each bar.
- Green represents a result above the selected line.
- A zero is displayed as a short red bar below the threshold.
- The dashed line, date, and opponent treatment match other field-player charts.
- Large one-match spikes remain visually obvious without introducing another chart type.

## Supporting stats

The visible defender supporting categories are:

- Minutes.
- Tackles.

Each category displays its average. The Minutes chart uses neutral gray bars and clearly exposes substitutions or limited appearances among full matches.

## Contextual right column

- Line Movement and Prop History retain the shared soccer layout.
- Matchup, Similar, Lineups, and Injuries remain the primary tabs.
- Odds, Stats, and Form remain the secondary tabs.
- The visible Matchup state uses team win probability and moneyline/handicap/total cards.
- The Averages card highlights the active Fouls average and provides Home, Away, and opponent samples.
